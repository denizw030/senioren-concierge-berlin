One product. Two usage models.

Standalone — or together with your concierge.

Phone reception can be used as a standalone service or combined with your personal concierge.

Standalone phone reception

A phone agent handles incoming calls for you. A personal concierge is not required for this.

  • Capture name and request
  • Take messages
  • Document conversations in a structured way

Concierge + phone reception

When used together, phone reception and your personal concierge can use the same context you have approved.

  • Answer calls in a structured way
  • Use information within the permitted context
  • You keep control of the next step
A moment of calm between the ring and your decision

Understand calls first. Then decide yourself.

Phone reception creates a clear first step: name, request and callback details are recorded before you decide whether and how you want to respond.

Name & request

The reason for the call is captured in a structured way instead of interrupting you without context.

Callback option

Contact details can be recorded so you can make your own decision later.

No pressure to decide

An incoming call does not automatically have to lead to immediate action.

Clear control

You decide which next step should actually happen.

Understand first. Then decide.NAHWERK does not replace your own judgement and does not guarantee reliable detection of problematic calls. Its value lies in providing a clear, calm first step.
One personal context

One concierge. The same conversation. Whatever the channel.

Your personal concierge and phone reception are designed to use the same personal context you have approved — instead of creating separate conversation worlds.

ExampleIn the morning you say: “If the property management calls, I’m only available after 5 pm.” Later, phone agent Alexander answers the call and can use this information within the permitted customer context. Afterwards, the message left by the caller is available to your personal concierge in the shared NAHWERK context.
Landline warm transfer

No smartphone required.

A normal landline phone is enough for the connection. No app, QR code, push notification or complicated technology is required.

1. Someone calls you2. NAHWERK answers3. Your landline phone rings4. Short private pre-call5. You decide about the connection
When you are unavailable: NAHWERK returns to the caller and can take a message instead of pretending a connection was made.
