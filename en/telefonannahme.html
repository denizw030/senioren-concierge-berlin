One concierge. One context.

What you tell FIDEL can help later on the phone.

Where enabled for your access, phone reception can use the same approved personal concierge context as your other STEWARO channels. It does not create a second, separate system.

Example

“If the property management calls, I will only be available after 5 pm today.”

Later, FIDEL can take this approved information into account when the call arrives.
