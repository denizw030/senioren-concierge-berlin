Standalone product

Phone reception — NAHWERK answers the phone for you.

Phone reception can be used independently of the personal concierge or combined with it. A phone agent can answer incoming calls, capture the caller's name and request, take messages and prepare a callback or connection when needed.

Keep using your existing number

You provide your existing landline number. We then coordinate the appropriate setup path with you.

One personal context

Your personal concierge and phone reception can work with the same customer context you have approved. No retelling, no unnecessary loss of information.

No smartphone required

For the connection, your normal landline phone can ring. No app, QR code or push notification is required.

For seniors — and for relatives.

NAHWERK is designed to support independence, not replace it. People arranging help for parents or grandparents therefore get as clear a starting point as those who use the concierge themselves.

Calm and clear

Short text blocks, clear steps and adaptable communication help handle even more complex topics without rushing.

Personal preferences

Preferred form of address, response style and useful communication preferences can be taken into account.

Relatives as a dedicated user group

NAHWERK can help prepare support for someone close to you without unnecessarily taking control of their everyday life.
