Simply use the phone

Your concierge stays the same on the phone.

When you call NAHWERK, you continue speaking with the personal concierge you selected — for example Hartmut. Alexander, by contrast, handles special phone tasks when NAHWERK calls somewhere on your behalf or call assistance is used.

  • Your chosen concierge remains your familiar voice
  • Alexander handles outbound phone tasks
  • Call assistance remains clearly separate from the personal concierge

More reassurance with unknown callers

NAHWERK phone reception can first screen unknown callers, ask for their name and reason for calling, take messages and route trusted contacts appropriately.

In enhanced protection mode, suspicious call patterns are handled especially cautiously — for example alleged family emergencies involving requests for money, supposed bank or police calls, requests for PINs, TANs or one-time codes, pressure to act quickly, secrecy, or requests for remote access, gift cards, cryptocurrency or collection of valuables.

Suspicious calls are not connected automatically and can instead be held back and reported. This is an additional layer of protection, not a guarantee that every fraud attempt will be detected.
