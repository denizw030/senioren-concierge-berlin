Frequently asked questions

Good to know.

Short, clear answers about NAHWERK, WhatsApp, personal support, relatives, AI and privacy.

What is NAHWERK?

NAHWERK is a personal, AI-supported concierge. The focus is clear support with everyday life, organisation, research, documents and communication — not the technology behind it.

How do I use NAHWERK?

WhatsApp is currently a particularly simple way to access NAHWERK. You can write naturally and send voice messages. No additional NAHWERK app is required for this access.

Is the concierge a human?

No. The digital concierges are AI-supported and are presented transparently as such. Human support is separate and must not be confused with the AI.

Can I get slower or simpler explanations?

Yes. You can ask for short answers, slower explanations or individual steps. Useful communication preferences can be taken into account within the intended scope.

Can I set up NAHWERK for my mother, father or grandparents?

NAHWERK treats relatives as a dedicated user group. The supported person should not be patronised; consent, transparency and control remain important.

Can NAHWERK explain photos or documents?

Where the relevant feature is enabled, submitted content can be reviewed and summarised clearly. AI can make mistakes; important information should be checked separately when necessary.

What does NAHWERK remember?

Useful preferences and context can be stored within the intended scope so everything does not have to be explained again each time. Correction and deletion follow the available functions and legal requirements.

Can a relative simply read private chats?

No, that is not the product principle. Administrative and Family functions should be controlled and permission-based; private communication must not be exposed unnecessarily.

Which plans and allowances currently apply?

You can find the current plans and allowances on the Plans page.

Does NAHWERK replace a doctor, care service, lawyer or tax adviser?

No. NAHWERK does not replace medical treatment, professional care, legal or tax advice, or emergency services.

How is data processed?

Different technical service providers may be used for the website, communication, automation, data storage and AI processing. Details are provided in the Privacy policy.

Can I have my data deleted?

Yes, provided there are no legal retention obligations or other legal grounds preventing it. The process is described under Data deletion described.
