A human touch in digital form

Technology should adapt to people — not the other way around.

NAHWERK Concierge follows a simple idea: digital support should be available through a familiar channel, explained clearly and tailored to the person. That is why Nilo or Mira works directly through WhatsApp.

Start free permanently

Familiar

No additional app and no new interface. Text, voice and photos are used where many people already communicate: in WhatsApp.

Clear

Nilo and Mira can explain things slowly, step by step and without unnecessary jargon. Users can ask for repetition or a slower speaking pace at any time.

More personal

Useful information such as devices, preferred form of address or communication preferences can be stored so you do not have to start from scratch with every question.

For seniors and relatives

Nilo and Mira are designed to support seniors with simple digital and everyday questions and reduce the burden on relatives when they cannot be there. A relative can set up access; the supported person is informed transparently about this.

Technology with clear boundaries
